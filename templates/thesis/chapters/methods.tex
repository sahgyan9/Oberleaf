\section{Theory}
Equations are numbered by chapter. Equation~\eqref{eq:schrodinger} is the
time-dependent Schr\"odinger equation:
\begin{equation}
  i\hbar\,\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\,\psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \hat{H}\,\psi(\mathbf{r}, t).
  \label{eq:schrodinger}
\end{equation}
