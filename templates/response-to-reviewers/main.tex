Dear Editor,

We thank the reviewers for their careful reading. Each comment is reproduced
below, followed by our reply and the corresponding change. Page and line
numbers refer to the revised manuscript.

\reviewer

\comment{The description of the calibration procedure is too brief to be
reproduced.}
\reply{We agree. Section 2.3 now gives the full procedure, including the
reference standard and the number of repetitions.}
\change{Page 4, lines 112--124: ``The detector was calibrated against a
reference source before each run...''}

\comment{Figure 3 lacks error bars.}
\reply{Error bars showing one standard deviation over five runs have been
added, and the caption states this.}
\change{Figure 3 and its caption, page 7.}

\reviewer

\comment{The authors should compare with the method of Smith et al.}
\reply{We have added this comparison as a new row in Table 2. Our method is
more accurate at a similar runtime.}
\change{Table 2, page 8; discussion on page 9, lines 201--209.}
